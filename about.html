Our Story

From one home to a thousand

Marie's Cleaning began over a decade ago with one woman, a bucket of eco-friendly supplies, and an obsession with corners other cleaners skipped. Word traveled — neighbour to neighbour, porch to porch.

Today, a trusted team serves homes and offices across the area. But the standard hasn't changed: every space is treated as if it were Marie's own.
